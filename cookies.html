Cookie Policy

This page explains what cookies and similar technology this site uses, and why.

Last updated: 6 August 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They're used by most websites to remember settings, keep things working correctly, or measure how a site is used.

What we use on this site

This site is intentionally lightweight. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it doesn't run any analytics scripts that profile visitors.

TypePurposeUsed here?
Essential / functionalBooking form, price estimator, page structureNo cookies needed — handled by your browser session only
AnalyticsUnderstanding visitor trafficNot currently used
AdvertisingTargeted adsNot used

Third-party services

When you submit a booking, the form sends your details directly to our database provider, Supabase, so we can store your booking. This is a direct data transfer needed to process your request — it isn't a tracking cookie, and Supabase doesn't use it to track you elsewhere.

Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers to display the site's typefaces. This may involve a standard request to Google's font delivery network, but no tracking cookie is set by this site as a result.

Your choices

Since this site doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies, there's nothing to opt out of. You can still control cookies generally through your browser settings if you'd like to block or clear any cookies from any site, including this one.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add analytics or other cookie-based tools in future, this page will be updated first and the "last updated" date above will change.
